# Play Together

A multiplayer game launcher for friends using phones as controllers and TVs or browsers as displays. Primary jobs: sign in, choose a real published game, create/join a room, pair devices and play. The owner explicitly asked for familiar console navigation, a single-screen layout and a redesign away from generic AI-generated cards. Google consent belongs in the browser, device approval is explicit, games stay immutable. Users test public main/production, not a local build.
